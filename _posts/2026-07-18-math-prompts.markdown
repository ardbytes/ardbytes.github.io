- What is a mathematical proof? What are its components? Give a worked example.
- Categorize the different types of proofs. Give examples of each type.
- What is the Well Ordering Principle? Construct a proof using it.
- Explain the SAT problem and its significance in computer science. Provide an
  example of a SAT problem and demonstrate how to solve it.
- What is a binary relation? List types of functions and their properties. Give
  examples of each type.
